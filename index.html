What it does

Every frame your console sends, and the ones in between

Console games often run at 30 fps, while your monitor can show 60, 90 or 120 pictures a second. SmoovPlay fills the gaps with frames drawn by AI from the real ones on either side, as you play.

4×Up to four times the frames: 30 fps becomes 60, 90 or up to 120.
9msTo draw each new frame, on an RTX 4070 Ti SUPER.
0Things to install on your console. Your controller connects to the PC.
$0Free and open source (MIT). No account, ads or tracking.
RIFE 4.22 lite

New frames, live

An open-source AI model draws 1, 2 or 3 frames between each pair the game sends, so the motion keeps up with your 60, 90 or 120 Hz screen.

FSR 1 · RTX VSR

Sharper on big screens

The 1080p stream is sharpened and scaled to your monitor by AMD FSR 1, in 0.1 ms, or NVIDIA RTX Video Super Resolution.

R · B

Instant replay

Press R to save the last 30 seconds to 5 minutes as an MP4, at the frame rate you see, with the game's sound. B saves a before & after video.

14 looks

Looks

Restyle the picture as you play, from Golden hour to Noir, or stack your own effects and share them as a short code.

Your home network

Works with what you have

A PS5 streams over Remote Play, through the open-source chiaki-ng. An Xbox through its app, or any console through a capture card, are Experimental options.

1 installer

Set up once, then it updates itself

Setup checks your PC, downloads the AI runtime from official sources and walks you through connecting your console: 10 to 20 minutes, once. New versions install in one click.

Good to know

Smoother, not faster

  • A little delayAbout 36 ms at 90 fps, or 20 ms at 60, on top of Remote Play's own. Great for driving, exploring and story games; not for competitive online play.
  • Made for 30 fps games60 fps games pass through unchanged, and games between 40 and 60 fps can be evened out (Experimental).
  • 1080p in, sharpened outRemote Play arrives at 1080p, so the picture is a little softer than HDMI straight from the console.
